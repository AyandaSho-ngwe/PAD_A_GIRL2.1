OUR SERVICES

Pad A Girl is committed to supporting girls and young women through menstrual products, education and community support.

WHAT WE DO

Explore our three main services.

Menstrual Product Donations

We provide sanitary products to girls who may not have access to essential menstrual hygiene products.

Menstrual Education

We educate young girls about menstrual health, hygiene, self-care and the importance of managing menstruation safely.

Community Support

We work with communities to create awareness, encourage donations and support girls in need.

Together, we can help girls stay confident, healthy and focused on their education.
