ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

Pad A Girl Foundation is a non-profit organisation dedicated to fighting menstrual poverty by providing sanitary products to girls and young women from disadvantaged communities.

The organisation was established to ensure that no girl misses school or opportunities because she cannot afford menstrual hygiene products. Through donations, partnerships, and awareness campaigns, the organisation empowers girls with dignity and access to menstrual health education.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is dedicated to making a difference and giving back to society. Our organisation aims to assist disadvantaged young girls with sanitary pads and also assist disadvantaged scholars with toiletries and school-related needs, along with providing food parcels to communities in need.

OUR VISION

We envision a future where no girl misses school or opportunities because of a lack of menstrual products or information.
