Pad A Girl is committed to empowering girls through menstrual health education, access to sanitary products and community support.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to ensure that every girl has access to the menstrual products and information she needs to feel confident, comfortable and empowered.

We believe that no girl should miss school or opportunities because she does not have access to sanitary products.

WHAT WE DO

Menstrual Products

Providing girls with access to essential menstrual products.

Education

Creating awareness and educating girls about menstrual health.

Community Support

Working together to create positive change in our communities.

TOGETHER WE CAN MAKE A DIFFERENCE

Every contribution, donation and act of support helps us empower another girl.
